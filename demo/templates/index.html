Pipeline

From field photo to unique identity in milliseconds

A single photograph passes through four stages of AI processing — each designed for accuracy, speed, and zero data leakage.

01
Capture
Upload field photography. Our detector locates every zebra in the frame with bounding box precision.
02
Detect & Classify
Species classifier distinguishes Plains from Grevy's zebras. Stripe region of interest is isolated for embedding.
03
Encode
MegaDescriptor backbone extracts a 512-d embedding, then PCA-compressed into a 256-bit Z-Hash binary code.
04
Match
Z-Hash is routed to federated FAISS shards. Only the 32-byte hash travels — never the original photograph.
Federation

Privacy-preserving cross-organization matching

Each conservation organization maintains sovereignty over their data. Only anonymized Z-Hash codes cross organizational boundaries.
